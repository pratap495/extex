EXTEK MANUFACTURING SERVICES

Aluminium Product Assembly

Our experienced team can advise on the most suitable fabrication solution. Aluminium fabrication shapes aluminium into finished products, such as solar-panel parts or outdoor enclosures.

Fabrication includes welding, stamping, bending, extruding and shearing. Extek Profiles has specialised in office partitioning, shower framework, lighting, automotive parts, fittings and fixtures.

The lightweight, strong and versatile nature of aluminium makes it suitable for products across almost every industry. We can explain options that may be more efficient, accurate or cost-effective for fit-for-purpose metal parts.

Service Highlights

  • ✓Welding: TIG or gas tungsten arc welding
  • ✓Stamping: custom components to exact specifications
  • ✓Bending: deformation into an angular shape
  • ✓Extruding: alloy material forced through a shaped die
  • ✓Product assembly and component fabrication
