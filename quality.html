OUR APPROACH TO QUALITY

Quality at every stage.

At Extek Profiles Private Limited, quality assurance focuses on material quality, dimensional accuracy and product inspection to meet applicable specifications and customer requirements.

Our process covers incoming raw materials, in-process checks and finished products.

QUALITY ASSURANCE

Checks at every stage
of production.

Our quality process focuses on materials, dimensions and finished products, against applicable specifications and customer requirements.

01

Raw Material Inspection

Assess incoming aluminium materials against specified requirements before they proceed to manufacturing.

02

Dimensional Accuracy Checks

Verify profiles and components against required measurements, drawings and design specifications.

03

Product Quality Inspection

Inspect finished aluminium products for visible defects and check that they meet the specified requirements.

04

Quality Testing

Assess product characteristics against applicable specifications and relevant quality requirements.

05

Certifications & Standards

Our certifications include ISO 9001:2015 for quality management, ISO 14001:2015 for environmental management, and ISO 45001:2018 for occupational health and safety.
