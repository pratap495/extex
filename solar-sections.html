Aluminium Solar Sections for Solar Energy Applications

Extek Profiles Pvt Ltd offers aluminium section solutions for suitable solar energy applications. Aluminium solar sections are used in solar mounting and support systems where lightweight materials, corrosion resistance and application-specific dimensions are important. The required profile design depends on the solar installation and its structural requirements.

Applications of Aluminium Solar Sections

Aluminium solar sections can be used in solar panel mounting structures, support frameworks and related solar installation systems, depending on the profile design and specifications. Their suitability depends on the mounting system, load requirements and installation conditions.
