WHO WE ARE

Your ideas.
Our aluminium expertise.

Extek Profiles Pvt Ltd is an aluminium extrusion and fabrication company based in Bibinagar, Telangana, India, providing aluminium solutions for industrial, commercial, and engineering applications. As a supplier of aluminium profiles and industrial aluminium sections, we offer a range of products, including aluminium pipes, tubes, rods, and plates to meet diverse manufacturing requirements. Our capabilities include aluminium extrusion, profile cutting, fabrication, and custom aluminium profile design based on specific dimensions and technical specifications. Our products serve various industries, including automotive, railway, heavy electrical, machinery manufacturing, power tools, motors and pumps. We focus on dimensional accuracy, product quality, and customer specifications to deliver suitable aluminium solutions for different applications. Our ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certifications reflect our commitment to quality management, environmental responsibility and occupational health and safety.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted name in the aluminium extrusion and fabrication industry by continuously improving our manufacturing capabilities, embracing innovation and maintaining high standards of quality. We aim to build long-term customer relationships by delivering dependable aluminium solutions that support industrial growth and changing market needs.

02.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide reliable aluminium extrusion, fabrication and customised aluminium profile solutions that meet customer specifications and industry requirements. We strive to maintain product quality, dimensional accuracy and consistent service while supporting the evolving needs of industrial, commercial and engineering applications.
