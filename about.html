WHO WE ARE

Your ideas.
Our aluminium expertise.

Extek Profiles provides aluminium fabrication services. Our skilled team plays a vital role in turning customers’ ideas into custom profile designs and finished parts that meet exacting specifications.

Aluminium extrusions are our core business. Committed to quality, we continuously strive for customer satisfaction and support our customers in their growth and success.

Aluminium is fascinating, flexible and versatile. Its journey from bauxite through production, use and recycling opens up possibilities for a wide range of applications.
