ABOUT EXTEK PROFILES

About Us

Extek Profiles Pvt Ltd is an ISO-certified aluminium extrusion manufacturer specialising in aluminium sections and profiles for industrial and commercial applications. Our product range includes aluminium profiles, pipes, tubes, rods, plates, and customised aluminium extrusion solutions to meet diverse industry requirements. We offer aluminium profile cutting, fabrication, and customised extrusion solutions with a focus on precision, product quality, and customer specifications. Backed by ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certifications, we are committed to quality management, environmental responsibility and workplace safety.
