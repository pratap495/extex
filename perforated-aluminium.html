EXTEK MANUFACTURING SERVICES

Applications and physical properties

Extek Profiles is a leading manufacturer of perforated aluminium sheet for a range of industries and applications. Pure aluminium is soft, ductile, corrosion resistant and highly electrically conductive.

Typical perforated aluminium applications include facades, balconies, balustrades, cladding, light fittings and baking trays.

Aluminium has a density around one third that of steel or copper, creating a high strength-to-weight ratio. Alloying elements such as manganese, silicon, copper and magnesium can increase strength and tailor properties for particular applications.
